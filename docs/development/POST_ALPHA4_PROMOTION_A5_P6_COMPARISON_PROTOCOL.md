## Purpose

Evaluate whether the bounded P6 competitive-falsification and factual-comparison infrastructure is eligible to continue as internal validation/tooling without becoming a source of semantic authority, external-validation claims, product ranking, or correctness substitution.

A5 is intentionally hostile to convenient comparison narratives. A factual matrix may retain asymmetry, unsupported lanes, negative evidence, and incomparability. It must not manufacture a winner.

## Fixed roles

1. **Innovation Scientist / Systems Architect** — preserve useful comparison power while keeping contracts proposition-scoped and non-authoritative.
2. **Anti-Drift / Scientific Integrity Reviewer** — freezes this corpus before execution and blocks score/ranking creep, evidence deletion, and post-result rule changes.
3. **Independent Falsifier / Red Team** — attacks authority laundering, outcome normalization, unsupported-to-negative conversion, source substitution, and negative-evidence erasure.
4. **Independent Critical-Milestone Reviewer** — independently verifies frozen source identities, retained failures, public anchors and final wording.

## Dynamic specialists

- scientific benchmarking / experimental design;
- CI reproducibility and provenance;
- comparative systems evaluation;
- adversarial evidence interpretation;
- Rust/runtime behavioral semantics;
- software supply-chain attestations.

## Frozen boundary

A5 may promote only **comparison/falsification tooling**. It does not promote a runtime feature and does not alter public alpha.4.

Required retained P6 facts include:
- decision `P6_FACTUAL_COMPARISON_COMPLETE_BOUNDED`;
- workload blob `c5986f1acd8bc79a3945e613acc128d5c8a9d168`;
- historical harness failure run `36770551463`;
- ExecSurface S2 `ERROR/INCOMPLETE`;
- Tetragon `NOT_TESTABLE_YET` as a coverage gap, not a negative product result;
- cicd-sensor native `passed` is not normalized to ExecSurface `PASS`;
- `NO_VALID_PERFORMANCE_COMPARISON` remains binding;
- P6 does not establish an overall winner, superiority/inferiority, full backend equivalence, universal privacy behavior, performance ranking, cross-platform parity, or public-release readiness.

## Acceptance rule

A5 may be `ELIGIBLE_BOUNDED` only if all frozen attacks/invariants pass in one candidate lineage and:

1. comparator-native labels never create ExecSurface semantic PASS/authority;
2. unsupported/not-tested/incomplete states cannot be rewritten as product absence, failure, or inferiority;
3. no global score, ranking, tier, winner or recommendation is introduced;
4. the no-performance-comparison decision remains intact unless a separately preregistered equivalent-boundary benchmark exists;
5. all frozen negative evidence remains locatable and unchanged in meaning;
6. source/workload pins and reproducibility evidence remain explicit;
7. P6 comparison results cannot satisfy P8 external-independence requirements;
8. P6 comparison output cannot substitute for Semantics-v3/P4/P5 correctness evidence;
9. immutable public alpha.4/stable `v0.1` anchors remain unchanged;
10. no public product crate is modified by A5.

No composite score and no partial-pass promotion.

## Kill criteria

Any reproducible case below blocks A5:
- comparator output becomes semantic authority or native PASS;
- missing/unsupported/not-tested evidence is interpreted as product failure/inability;
- a winner/ranking/composite score is introduced;
- negative evidence is removed, hidden or relabeled;
- non-equivalent performance measurements are ranked;
- P6 is used as independent external validation or P8 evidence;
- comparison replaces proposition-scoped correctness evidence;
- source/workload identity becomes unbound;
- public v2 semantics, alpha.4 or stable tag is changed.
