## Why the outcome is EPHEMERAL_ONLY

A broader `P3_VARIANCE_MODEL_VALUE_ESTABLISHED_BOUNDED` decision would overstate the evidence.

The evidence supports a narrow mechanism:
1. a producer-specific randomized identity was independently evidenced;
2. the classifier required actor/role/causal constraints rather than path shape alone;
3. the projection removed the targeted identity variance on six fresh checks;
4. non-target behavior remained visible;
5. an injected meaningful drift sentinel remained unseen/residual;
6. the declared falsification suite found no false-PASS path.

The evidence does **not** support automatic authorization of general multi-run variability:
- the explicit accepted-variable set remained empty;
- recurrence remained non-authorizing;
- 102 non-target variable candidates remained visible in the corrected learning set;
- R4 found no check where the explicit-variance layer reduced residual findings below the bounded ephemeral projection.

Therefore the product-value conclusion is deliberately narrower than the full research architecture.

## Product boundary after P3

Authorized for future integration research:
- evidence-backed producer-specific ephemeral-identity projection as a narrow semantic stabilization mechanism;
- only under explicit actor/role/causal/root grammar;
- only with raw/pre-projection evidence retained;
- only with non-target preservation and false-PASS sentinel tests;
- only through a separate integration/release gate.

Not authorized:
- broad `$TMP`, cache, module, stdlib or workspace normalization;
- automatic union/majority/frequency-based acceptance;
- automatic baseline mutation;
- public use of the research clone/fd certificate;
- reinterpretation of public alpha.4/v2 semantics;
- a general claim that P3 solved build nondeterminism.
