## Product interpretation

### Established

The evidence supports retaining the narrow architectural mechanism:

`evidence-backed ephemeral identity classification -> bounded canonical projection`

for cases where the producer/role/grammar/provenance contract is independently established and falsified.

This mechanism can reduce identity-only nondeterministic noise without silently authorizing unrelated variable behavior in the declared test scope.

### Not established

P3 does not support promoting any of the following as product semantics:
- frequency-based authorization
- automatic acceptance of recurrent variable behavior
- broad `$TMP`, cache, module, stdlib, workspace or source-tree suppression
- the 102 observed non-target variable candidates
- generic multi-run variance as a PASS-producing mechanism
- public certificate-aware ptrace completion under raw v2

The multi-run analyzer and accepted-variance contract remain useful research/falsification tools, but they are not promoted to automatic product authorization from P3.

## Integration boundary

P3 closes at the research/product-decision level only.

A later release/integration gate may evaluate a narrow implementation of the bounded ephemeral classification/projection together with the required evidence contract, compatibility/migration analysis and public fail-closed regression suite.

Until that separate gate closes:
- `v0.1.0-alpha.4` remains unchanged;
- stable `@v0.1` remains unchanged;
- `main` receives no P3 runtime integration;
- certificate-aware completion remains research-only;
- P3 research failures remain part of the permanent evidence record.
