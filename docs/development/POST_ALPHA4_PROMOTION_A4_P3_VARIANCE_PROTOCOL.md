## Purpose

Attempt to promote only the bounded GCC ephemeral-identity projection that survived prior P3 research. This gate is designed to fail if variance handling becomes authorization, hides meaningful drift, broadens beyond the frozen grammar/role contract, or mutates raw evidence.

This is not a learned-baseline promotion, not a generic similarity system, and not authority derived from frequency.

1. **Innovation Scientist / Systems Architect** — seek the smallest useful variance projection with the strongest semantic boundary.

## Frozen candidate boundary

Only the existing bounded GCC temp identity contract may be evaluated:

- actor path exactly `/usr/bin/gcc`;
- actor family exactly `gcc`;
- actor is the terminal execution-chain member;
- temp basename satisfies the existing exact GCC ephemeral grammar;
- the same identity has a create+write open and delete role;
- conflicting use, rename involvement, wrong actor, wrong root, malformed grammar or missing role makes the identity ineligible;
- the projection is a derived candidate view only; the raw input remains unchanged.

No broad regex, similarity threshold, frequency threshold, learning rule or observed-popularity rule may be introduced by A4.

## Acceptance rule

A4 may be `ELIGIBLE_BOUNDED` only if **all** of the following hold in one frozen candidate lineage:

1. measurable false-REVIEW reduction is demonstrated on a bounded benign GCC ephemeral pair;
2. the exact frozen A4 adversarial corpus passes with **zero new false PASS**;
3. meaningful non-ephemeral drift remains visible after projection;
4. repetition/frequency never converts an ineligible observation into eligible variance;
5. raw source evidence remains unchanged after candidate projection;
6. prior P3 falsifiers and the repaired Semantics-v3 authority boundary remain green;
7. public alpha.4 and stable `v0.1` remain pinned to `48e0b9a0707553349e75e97a9dfa096d13f9ab5d`.

No composite score and no partial-pass promotion.

## Kill criteria

Any reproducible case below blocks P3 promotion:

- an ineligible identity is projected because it was frequent/repeated;
- wrong actor/root/grammar/role or a collision is projected;
- a meaningful non-ephemeral behavioral change disappears after projection;
- applying the candidate mutates or replaces raw evidence;
- projection outcome depends on effect ordering;
- public v2 semantics/tags are reinterpreted;
- prior negative evidence is deleted or relabeled.
